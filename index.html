FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our athletic gear, sizing, and availability.

Q: What does "PRGRSSV" stand for?

A: PRGRSSV represents "Progressive" symbolizing a commitment to continuous self-improvement, physical growth, and a relentless work ethic inside and outside the gym.

Q: Where are your products made?

A: As a starting brand, we source our blank apparel from trusted local suppliers, while all printing and customization are done in-house using heat press technology.

Q:How do I place an order?

A: This website functions as our official digital showcase and look book. For inquiries you can place an order directly by sending us a message on our official Facebook Page with your selected items, size, and shipping details.

Q: How should I care for and wash the activewear?

A: Machine wash cold inside-out with like colors. Tumble dry low or air dry. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners to maintain elasticity and moisture-wicking coatings.
